# Sweet Lift Taxi Demand Forecasting

## Project Overview
Developed a Time series forecasting model for Sweet Lift Taxi Company to predict the number of taxi orders for the next hour. The goal was to help the company anticipate periods of high demand and attract more drivers during peak hours.

## Business Objective
Sweet Lift Taxi Company collected historical data on taxi orders at airports. The objective was to build a machine learning model capable of accurately forecasting hourly taxi demand so the company could better plan driver avaliability.

## Approach
- Prepared and resampled historical taxi order data into hourly intervals.
- Explored the time series to identify trends and patterns in taxi demand.
- Created time-based and lag features to improve forecasting performance.
- Trained and compared predictive models for hourly taxi demand.
- Evaluated model performance using Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE).

## Results
The final model achieved an **RMSE of approximately 44.09**, successfully meeting the project's performance requirement. 

The model demonstrated the ability to forecast short-term taxi demand and could help Sweet Lift Taxi Company better anticipate periods when additional drivers may be needed.
