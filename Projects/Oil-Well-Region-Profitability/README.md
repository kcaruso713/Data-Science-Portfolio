# Oil Well Region Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview
Developed a machine learning solution to identify the most profitable region for new oil well development. Analyzed oil reserve data from three regions and trained linear regression models to estimate potential reserves and support data-driven investment decisions.

## Objectives
- Analyze oil well data across three potential development regions.
- Train linear regression models to predict oil reserves.
- Compare predicted production potential across regions.
- Evaluate profitability and risk to determine the strong region for investment.

## Approach
Prepared and analyzed regional oil well, trained linear regression models to predict reserve volumes, and evaluated model performance. Predicted reserves were then incorporated into profitability analysis to compare potential development opportunities and identify the region offering the strongest business potential.
