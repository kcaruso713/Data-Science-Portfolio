# Customer Churn Prediction
## Project Overview
Developed an end-to-end machine learning solution to predict customer churn and identify customers at increased risk ofleaving, supporting data-driven customer retention decisions.

## Business Objective
The goal of this project was to build a predictive model that could identify customers likely to churn, allowing the business to proactively target at-risk customers with retention strategies.

## Approach
- Explored and analyzed customer data to identify patterns associated with churn.
- Cleaned and prepared data for machine learning, including preprocessing and feature engineering.
- Trained and compared multiple classification models, including Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and LightGBM.
- Evaluated model performance using ROC-AUC and other classification metrics.
- Applied class weighting and model optimization to improve predictive performance.

## Results
LightGBM produced the strongest overall performance, achieving approximately **0.89 ROC-AUC in cross-validation** and approximately **0.90 ROC-AUC on the final test set**.
The final model demonstrated a strong ability to distinguish between customers likely to remain and customers at increased risk of churn.
